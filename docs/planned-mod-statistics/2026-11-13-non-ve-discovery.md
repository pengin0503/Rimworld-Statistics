Companion to the [eight third-wave candidates](2026-11-13-thirdwave-candidate-register.md), which remain separate from the original 35-candidate backlog. **No new metric has been implemented in this research.** Source audits used published repositories and descriptions available on 2026-11-13; **a 1.7 Workshop tag is not proof of the loaded assembly or of the hook.** Ranking prioritizes accurate, unique lifetime event counters over snapshots or hot-path polling.

### Implementation order

1. **F01 Vehicle Framework**: one high-coverage framework source instead of per-vehicle-variant hooks. Its explicit `UpgradeCompleted` event could also serve E06 (Vanilla Vehicles Expanded – Upgrades) once the upgrade's identifying Def is recoverable.
2. **F02 Roads of the Rim**: exact distinct world-map infrastructure commit path in 1.7 source. Reliable success definition and no normal vanilla construction duplication.
3. **F03 Hospitality: Spa**: source has a compact silver transfer boundary; **payment** should be the first slice, not "completed spa use".
4. **F04 Hospitality: Vending machines**: attractive item-category sales data, but **first obtain true 1.7 loaded assembly** and guard actual movement of goods and silver.
5. **F05 Altered Carbon**: high-value, unusual life-cycle achievements; **defer until actual 1.7 assembly selection is verified**, given public loadFolder mismatch.
6. **F06–F08**: only promote when active binary/source and an inexpensive confirmed outcome exist.

### Audited exclusions / defer instead of counting proxies

- **Research Reinvented** (`PeteTimesSix.ResearchReinvented`) [source](https://github.com/PeteTimesSix/ResearchReinvented) — `About.xml` and `LoadFolders.xml` explicitly end at **1.6**; no verified active 1.7 release. Research points/projects also largely overlap the existing host. **Hold**.
- **Questionable Ethics Enhanced (Continued)** (`Mlie.QuestionableEthicsEnhanced`) [source](https://github.com/emipa606/QuestionableEthicsEnhanced) — public `About.xml` ends at **1.6**. Successful vat clones are potentially distinct, but the 1.7 proof is missing. **Hold**.
- **More Faction Interaction (Continued)** [source](https://github.com/emipa606/MoreFactionInteraction) — the inspected distribution ends at **1.6**, despite its attractive dedicated diplomacy/exposition/world-action events. **Hold until supported 1.7 build**.
- **Standalone inventory/crafting/item-only content packs** — already largely eligible for host generic ThingDef-based totals; don't add redundant families.
- **Cosmetic, GUI, renderer, pathfinder and pure performance MODs** — generally no independent in-world outcome worth collecting.
- **Hotels and Recreation source caution:** the public [`ptpatch/HotelsAndRecreation`](https://github.com/ptpatch/HotelsAndRecreation) repo dates from **2021** and is **not evidence of the active 2026 Workshop implementation** (E08). Exact current source/assembly is unresolved.

Before implementation, inspect actual RimWorld 1.7 selected source/binary, confirm package ID and dependencies, identify the successful commit and counter unit, de-duplicate against existing checkpoint/generic families, add tests, and check per-tick overhead. The owner's previous permission to **skip difficult-to-verify statistics** applies; record a waiver rather than a proxy. No release or Workshop changes made by this document.
