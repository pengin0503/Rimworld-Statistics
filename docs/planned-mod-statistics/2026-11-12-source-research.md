## Previously investigated candidates: important source anchors

- [VFE Factory — `Building_Autofarmer.ProcessRow`](https://github.com/Vanilla-Expanded/VanillaFurnitureExpanded-Factory/blob/main/1.6/Source/Things/Building_Autofarmer.cs): creates harvested item stacks directly (`ThingMaker.MakeThing`), stores them or attempts `GenPlace.TryPlaceThing`, and sows using `GenSpawn.Spawn`. Does not automatically pass the vanilla pawn harvest callback. Capture only committed outputs/plant spawns. `v1.7` loadFolders selects `1.6`.
- [VFE Insectoids 2 — `CompHive.TrySpawnPawn`](https://github.com/Vanilla-Expanded/VanillaFactionsExpanded-Insectoids2/blob/main/1.7/Source/Hives/CompHive.cs): `PawnGenerator.GeneratePawn`, `AddInsect`, then `GenSpawn.Spawn`. Count **after** successful spawn, gated to player hives; skip rehydrated/inherited insects. Real `1.7/Source` exists.
- [VQE Drone Factory — `Building_AutomatedDroneAssembler`](https://github.com/Vanilla-Expanded/VanillaQuestsExpanded-DroneFactory/blob/main/1.6/Source/Buildings/Building_AutomatedDroneAssembler.cs) and [`CompDroneHatcher.Hatch`](https://github.com/Vanilla-Expanded/VanillaQuestsExpanded-DroneFactory/blob/main/1.6/Source/Comps/CompDroneHatcher.cs): generation and spawn flows exist. The assembler and hatcher may describe the **same** manufactured drone; don't double count. `v1.7` loads `1.6`.
- [Vanilla Aspirations Expanded — `Need_Fulfillment.Complete`](https://github.com/Vanilla-Expanded/VanillaAspirationsExpanded/blob/main/1.7/Source/Needs/Need_Fulfillment.cs): returns immediately for unknown/already completed aspiration, stores `completedTicks`, increments `CurLevel`; an available candidate for exactly-once completion-by-Def event.
- [VFE Pirates source](https://github.com/Vanilla-Expanded/VanillaFactionsExpanded-Pirates/tree/main/1.7/Source): verify actual warcasket/hiring completion methods. Other Tier 1 candidate IDs are **proposals only** until similar evidence is established.

This is a research ranking, **not** a release schedule. All target absence, save/load cycles, version mismatch, repeated invocation and multi-colony attribution need tests.
